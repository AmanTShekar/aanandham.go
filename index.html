★ STEPS

Make it happen in
just four steps

We’ve made the entire process smooth and hassle-free. Our mountain team guides you through every step from booking to summit — just show up ready for the peaks.

01

Choose your package & trail

We have three flexible options: Ridge Glamp (double dome pods for couples or solo explorers), Summit Trek (high-altitude peak climbs), and Squad Camp (group suite tents for friends). Pick what fits your budget and travel style.

02

Leave your contact & trekker details

Fill out a quick form with your preferred dates, group size, fitness level, and equipment requirements. Our mountain expedition manager will reach out within 24 hours to confirm forest permits and guide you.

03

Complete payment & get welcome kit

Once confirmed, secure your spot with hassle-free payment. You will immediately receive a welcome pack with trail maps, packing lists, weather advisory, and be added to your batch WhatsApp group.

04

Reach basecamp & conquer the peaks

Arrive at our basecamp in Munnar or Wayanad. Our 4x4 Jeep convoy and certified Wilderness First Aid marshals take care of all gear, meals, campfire setups, and guided summit trails!

01 Do I need to know how to trek or camp already?

Not at all! We offer beginner-friendly ridge glamping and scenic walks as well as moderate to challenging summit climbs. Every expedition is guided by certified Wilderness First Aid trek marshals who pace the hike according to the group’s comfort.

02 I'm traveling alone — will I feel out of place?

Over 45% of our campers and trekkers join solo! Our community-driven Strangers Camp format, shared campfire BBQ, and group icebreaker games make it effortless to meet like-minded nature enthusiasts and build lasting friendships.

03 What's not included in the price?

The package covers your campsite accommodation, all meals (BBQ dinner + morning breakfast), 4x4 Jeep transfers, guide fees, and forest department entry permits. Personal travel to the basecamp meeting point and personal porterage are not included.

04 What if the dates don't work for me?

We organize custom private batches for groups of 4 or more on any weekday or weekend of the year! Simply reach out via our Custom Arrangements form or WhatsApp, and we will tailor dates and itineraries for you.

05 Is the wilderness camp safe for solo female travelers?

100% yes. We enforce strict zero-tolerance safety policies, maintain 24/7 on-site female and male camp coordinators, provide private enclosed western washrooms, and station staff at all perimeter points throughout the night.

06 Can I extend my stay at the ridge glamp?

Absolutely! You can easily extend your stay at our tea estate glamping pods or combine a high-altitude Kolukkumalai sunrise trek with Vagamon paragliding or Wayanad bamboo rafting. Let us know during booking!
